World Models for Adaptive Quadruped Locomotion

When a quadruped steps from firm ground into mud, the same joint command can produce a different motion. A foot can sink under load and meet resistance as it pulls free. I use a transformer to encode recent joint commands and the motion they produced, then condition a latent dynamics model on that history. A model predictive path integral controller uses the model to evaluate candidate command sequences as new observations arrive. In simulation, recent history improved motion predictions when friction or actuator strength changed. I also study how those predictions affect control during transitions onto deformable terrain.

Closed-Loop-Compliant Reinforcement Learning

A robot holding a tool may need to yield at its elbow when pushed while keeping its hand on target. I post-train an existing policy using paired simulations that start from the same state, with an external force applied to one robot. The difference between their task-space motions isolates the response to the force. I train a residual policy over the frozen base policy to match that response to a virtual mass–spring–damper model, which specifies how the robot should yield and recover after release. I use the resulting method to study how robots can accommodate contact while retaining their original skills.
