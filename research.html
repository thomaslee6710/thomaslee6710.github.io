Research

LDM-Quad

I use a transformer to summarize recent commands and observed motion. A latent dynamics model uses that history to predict how a quadruped will move under candidate commands, and a model predictive controller uses those predictions to adapt to changes such as the transition from firm ground to mud.

CLC-RL

I developed a post-training method that gives an existing policy a tunable response to an external force. I compare paired rollouts with and without the force to isolate the motion it causes, then train a residual policy to match that motion to a target mass–spring–damper response while the robot performs its task.
