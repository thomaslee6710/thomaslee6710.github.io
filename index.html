About me

I’m a master’s student in robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, where I’m advised by Prof. Aaron Johnson in the Robomechanics Lab. I earned my bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering at CMU, with an additional major in robotics and a minor in business administration.

I work at the intersection of reinforcement learning and optimal control, with an interest in building world models from robot interaction data and using them to plan and control motion in contact-rich tasks.
